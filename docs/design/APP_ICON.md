# Macro App Icon

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The production asset is
`apps/ios/Macro/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`.

The icon uses an off-white bitten apple on deep emerald. The square image has
no baked-in corner mask; iOS supplies the rounded icon shape. The generated
artwork was resized to the 1024 x 1024 app-icon asset size without other edits.

## Generation Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one final production iOS app-icon bitmap for a minimalist nutrition-tracking app named Macro. Square 1024 x 1024 canvas, full bleed opaque deep emerald green background (#104C3E) with an extremely restrained tonal variation, mostly flat. Center a large off-white (#F5F2E8) stylized edible apple fruit with a clearly visible small bite taken from its upper-right edge. Make an original friendly sculptural fruit silhouette: broad rounded plump body, gentle lobes, short curved stem, and two small unequal leaves, distinctly a food/nutrition symbol rather than the Apple computer company mark. Bold simple silhouette, exceptionally clean smooth edges, balanced negative space, sophisticated editorial minimalism. The whole fruit including stem and leaves should occupy approximately 66 percent of the canvas width and 72 percent of its height, optically centered. Only emerald and off-white; no lettering, no wordmark, no border, no device mockup, no extra objects, no photorealism, no glass reflections, no heavy shadows. Background reaches every corner. Do not round the square canvas corners: iOS will apply its own icon mask. Output exactly one finished icon, not a presentation sheet.
